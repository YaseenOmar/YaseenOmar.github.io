Place your portfolio screenshots in this folder with the following filenames to enable visual previews:

1. profile-placeholder.webp      - Your professional avatar square photo.
2. project-cashiri.webp           - Cashiri point of sale screenshot.
3. project-weather.webp           - Weather app screen screenshot.
4. project-athkar.webp            - Athkar remembrance screen screenshot.
5. project-electricity.webp       - Electricity meter reading screenshot.
6. project-paging.webp            - Jetpack paging demo screenshot.
7. social-preview.webp            - Main card image used for social share link embeds.

If these webp files are missing, the website will automatically hide broken image icons and fall back to custom CSS gradients.
